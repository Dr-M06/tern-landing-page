Delete your account

Last updated: 8 August 2026

The short version

Email us with your Tern ID. We confirm, then delete your account and the server data listed below within 30 days. Chat history on phones is not something we can wipe remotely.

How to request deletion

  1. Find your Tern ID Open Tern and go to Settings. Your Tern ID is the eight-character code at the top, for example BA78A478.
  2. Email us Write to support-tern@niilox.com with the subject Delete my account and your Tern ID in the body.
  3. We confirm and delete We reply to confirm. Your account and the data listed below are deleted within 30 days.

What happens to your data

Deleted

  • Your account record and Tern ID
  • Your public encryption keys
  • Messages still waiting to be delivered
  • Your profile photo
  • Media and files held on our servers
  • Your push notification token

Kept

  • Reports about your account, and any ban — kept for 12 months so a banned Tern ID cannot be reset by deleting and signing up again
  • Basic server logs, kept for 30 days

What we cannot delete

Messages you already sent live on the recipient’s phone. Tern never held a readable copy of them, so we cannot remove them from someone else’s device. Your own chat history is stored only on your phone — clearing the app or uninstalling removes it.
