Privacy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

The short version

We cannot read your messages. They are encrypted on your phone before they reach us, and we do not hold the key. We do not ask for your phone number or your contacts. We do not show ads, and we do not sell anything about you to anyone.

We do store some things, because a messaging service cannot work otherwise. That list is below, and it is the whole list.

Who we are

Tern is operated by Nomli Tech Limited, a company registered in Nigeria. For anything in this policy, write to dev@niilox.com.

What we store

  • Your sig. The eight-character handle you share with people. Kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Your device token. A random secret your app holds to prove it is you. We store only a hash of it, never the token itself.
  • Your Google account identifier and email address — only if you choose Google as your recovery method. Used for one purpose: giving you your account back on a new phone.
  • A push notification token from Apple or Google, so your phone can be woken when a message arrives. Kept while your account exists or until you turn wakes off.
  • Messages we have not yet delivered, as encrypted bytes we cannot read, plus the sender’s and recipient’s account ids and a timestamp so we know where to send them. Deleted the moment they are delivered, and discarded after 30 days if they never are.
  • Encrypted files. Photos, documents and profile pictures are encrypted before upload and stored as bytes we cannot read. Photos and documents are deleted after 30 days. Profile pictures are deleted after a year of inactivity or when you delete your account.
  • Your public encryption keys (identity and optional signing key, and an optional avatar descriptor), so other people can message you securely. Kept while your account exists.
  • Connection records, including your IP address, kept for 14 days to detect abuse and keep the service running.
  • Anything you report to us. When you report a person or conversation, your app may send us the reported content and nearby messages in readable form, because you chose to show them to us. Kept for 12 months, or longer if needed for an ongoing safety or legal matter.

What we never have

  • The contents of your messages, voice notes, photos or documents, unless you report them to us.
  • Your phone number. Tern does not use one.
  • Your contacts or address book. The app does not request permission to read them, and your nicknames for people never leave your phone.
  • Your location. We do not collect it, and photos are re-encoded before sending, which removes location metadata a camera may store in them.
  • Any analytics or advertising code. There is none in the app.

What stays on your phone

Your chat history, your nicknames for people, payment details you save, and any media you have downloaded live only on your device. We hold no copy. If you lose your phone, that history is gone — and if you move to a new phone, your account can come back but your old conversations do not.

Push notifications

To wake your phone we send a signal through Apple or Google. It carries no message content — those companies learn that something arrived for your device, not what it was or who sent it.

Where your data is

Our messaging and identity servers run in the European Union (nlite.niilox.com). Encrypted file storage used by the app is also in the European Union. If you live outside the EU, your data is processed in the EU when you use Tern.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, ask us to send it somewhere else, or object to how we use it. Write to dev@niilox.com and we will respond within 30 days.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In the European Union you may contact your local supervisory authority; in Finland that includes the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman. In Nigeria, the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Deleting your account

Delete your account from Settings in the app when that control is available, or write to dev@niilox.com from the email linked to your recovery (if any) and ask us to close the account. Closing removes your sig, device token hash, recovery link, undelivered messages, public keys and profile picture from our systems. Messages you already sent to other people stay on their phones, because we cannot reach into someone else’s device. Reports and connection records are kept for the periods above.

Children

Tern is not for anyone under 13. If you are under 18, check your local law and get a parent’s permission before using it. If we learn that an account belongs to a child under 13, we delete it.

Security

Messages are end-to-end encrypted. Files are encrypted with a key that only you and the person you sent them to ever hold. Connections to our servers are encrypted in transit. No system is perfect, and we will tell you if something happens that affects you.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
